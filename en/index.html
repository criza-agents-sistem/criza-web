The name

CRIZA comes from chrysalis.

The passage from science to industry is a metamorphosis. While it's happening, it doesn't have the beauty of the final form — it demands method, work and stages. But that's what makes what emerges — a technology, a product, a business — come out strong enough to fly. Our place is inside that process, through every stage — implementation included.

What we do

One table, every capability a development needs

We start from a concrete problem and analyze it from every angle — technical, scientific, environmental, economic and commercial. From there we identify opportunities that usually stay hidden, and build the solution and the business from the earliest stages.

How we work

In stages, with decision milestones

The engagement moves forward in successive stages. Each one has its own objectives, deliverables, budget and decision milestone. We move forward when the results justify it — and any stage can be reshaped or discontinued at its close.

  1. 01

    Technical and commercial feasibility 90 days

    Determine whether there's a technical and business opportunity solid enough to justify a proof of concept.

    MilestoneContinue, reframe the search, or close.

  2. 02

    Proof of concept 3–6 months

    Demonstrate, in a lab or controlled setting, that the chosen path works technically and economically.

    MilestoneA validated, reproducible path.

  3. 03

    Industrial pilot 6–12 months

    Validate the solution under real operating conditions and determine whether it can become a viable industrial unit.

    MilestoneSuccessful pilot.

  4. 04

    First implementation

    Design, finance and implement the first commercial unit at industrial scale.

    MilestoneUnit in operation.

  5. 05

    Scale-up and commercialization

    Turn the first validated experience into a solution that's replicable across other plants and markets.

    MilestoneReplication or license.

The model

A partner in the development and in the business

Role

We join as a strategic partner: project direction, coordination of the scientific and technological capabilities, economic analysis and scale-up strategy.

Fees

By stage, with independent budgets. Both parties' commitment grows as results come in.

Risk

Shared. CRIZA takes a stake in the technology and the business that come out of the joint work.

Decision

Always the client's. The system builds and proposes; the choice belongs to whoever brings the case.
